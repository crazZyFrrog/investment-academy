# What Is Investing?

Investing is the act of allocating capital today with the expectation of future financial return.

<Callout type="insight">
Saving protects purchasing power. Investing grows it over long horizons.
</Callout>

## Core idea

When you invest, you accept uncertainty in exchange for potential growth. The key is matching that uncertainty to your timeline and goals.

<Formula>
Expected Return ≈ Risk Premium + Time × Compounding
</Formula>

## Takeaway

Start with clarity on **why** you invest, then choose instruments that fit your horizon.
