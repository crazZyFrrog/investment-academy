# Risk and Return

Higher potential return usually comes with higher volatility. There is no free lunch in markets.

<Callout type="warning">
Chasing recent winners is one of the most common ways beginners destroy long-term outcomes.
</Callout>

## The tradeoff

- Lower risk assets tend to move less, but may grow more slowly.
- Higher risk assets can compound faster, but drawdowns are part of the journey.

## Practical frame

Build a portfolio you can hold through stress. Consistency beats prediction.
